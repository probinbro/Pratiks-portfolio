02 — How I work

From pixel to decision

Every project I run follows the same three moves. The last one is the one most remote-sensing work skips.

STEP 01

Acquire

Landsat and Sentinel archives, station records, terrain and climate covariates — assembled in Google Earth Engine so the whole stack is reproducible from a single script rather than a folder of exports.

STEP 02

Classify & model

Random Forest, XGBoost and SVM benchmarked against each other, fuzzy clustering for regionalisation, AHP where expert weighting beats a black box. Accuracy is reported honestly, including where the model fails.

STEP 03

Ground-truth & publish

Field visits to verify what the classifier claims, then a paper with the workflow written out and the script shared. If a junior researcher can't rerun it next week, the study isn't finished.
