Rainfall climatology · Bangladesh · Fuzzy clustering

Homogenous rainfall regions across Bangladesh

Innovative trend-analysis technique using fuzzy logic and K-means clustering to classify homogenous rainfall regions from 40 years of records across 29 BMD stations. The workflow reproducibly identifies zones with similar rainfall dynamics, supporting downstream water-resource and agricultural planning.

Objective

Identify climatologically homogenous rainfall zones for regional planning.

Methods

Fuzzy c-means, K-means, innovative trend analysis in R.

Outcome

Cluster map and trend indices used for policy briefings.

Featured site: Tanguar Haor

Tanguar Haor is a seasonally inundated freshwater wetland complex in Sunamganj district, north-east Bangladesh. Ramsar-designated in 2000, it supports rice cultivation, fisheries, and migratory bird populations, and provides critical flood-buffering ecosystem services. Field visits have contributed ground-truth samples for classification accuracy and community observations that anchor the modelling in lived experience.
